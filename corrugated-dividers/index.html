How PP Corrugated Dividers Are Made

Dividers start as flat 48×96 in PP corrugated sheet stock, then:

  • Cut to the box interior height and width
  • Slotted at regular intervals to half-depth
  • Assembled by interlocking slots — no adhesive needed
  • Cell count and size configured to product dimensions

Specification Guide for Corrugated Plastic Boxes

Choosing the right corrugated plastic boxes requires matching wall thickness, flute profile, and resin grade to your specific application. Key specifications:

  • Wall thickness: 4mm for light duty, 6mm for standard, 8mm for heavy duty, 10mm for bulk container applications
  • Flute type: B-flute for top-load stacking strength, C-flute for balanced performance, X-flute (double wall) for maximum rigidity
  • Resin grade: Standard PP for ambient environments, high-density PP for cold chain and freezer applications down to -40°F
  • Food-grade: FDA 21 CFR-compliant virgin PP for direct food contact — required for produce, poultry, seafood, and processed food applications
